project name : Academeet

Featues:-

## **1. User & Profile Management**

* Sign up with university email (verification).
* Create a profile: name, department, semester, skills, interests.
* Role toggle: Mentee (default) / Mentor / Both.
* Edit profile anytime.
* Option to become a mentor or disable mentor mode.

---

## **2. Mentorship Directory**

* Search and browse mentors by:

  * Department, skills, course, availability, rating, mode (online/in-person).
* Mentor profiles include:

  * Skills, bio, availability, ratings/reviews, past sessions.
* Book a mentor directly for a session.
* Availability calendar to pick time slots.

---

## **3. Help Requests (Marketplace)**

* Mentees can post “Need Help With…” requests:

  * Title, description, course, preferred time, optional budget.
* Mentors can browse requests and **offer help**:

  * Propose time, fee (if applicable), message.
* Mentees review offers and accept one.
* Request statuses: Open, Assigned, Closed.
* Mentor offers statuses: Pending, Accepted, Declined.

---

## **4. Booking & Sessions**

* Confirmed sessions (from Help Request or direct booking).
* Session info: mentor, mentee, time, duration, mode.
* Booking statuses: Scheduled, Completed, Cancelled, No-show.
* Integration with calendar (optional).

---

## **5. Reviews & Ratings**

* After session, mentee rates mentor (1–5 stars).
* Short feedback + tags (e.g., “Explains clearly”, “On time”).
* Public display on mentor profile to build credibility.

---

## **6. MySpace (Personal Dashboard)**

* Unified hub for both mentor and mentee roles.
* **Sections:**

  * Upcoming sessions (mentee + mentor view)
  * Requests:

    * Mentee → My posted Help Requests
    * Mentor → Incoming requests and offers made
  * History: Completed sessions and reviews
  * Notifications: All updates, reminders, new offers, session changes
  * Profile & settings: Edit personal info, role toggle, availability

---

## **7. Notifications & Reminders**

* Automated notifications for:

  * New requests / offers
  * Bookings confirmed / cancelled
  * Reminders (24h / 1h before session)
  * Reviews requested
* Settings for email/push notifications.

---

## **8. Admin Features**

* Approve/reject “Mentor of the Month” banner.
* Moderate reviews (if necessary).
* View platform metrics: top mentors, active users, sessions per week.

---

## **Optional / Future Enhancements**

* Paid sessions & transaction tracking (if you want a monetization model).
* Featured mentors & trending Help Requests on home page.
* Gamification: badges for most active mentors or mentees.
